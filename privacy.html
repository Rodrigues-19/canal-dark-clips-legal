Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Canal Dark Clips is a private, single-user application. It has no other users and does not collect data from the public.

Data handled

When the owner signs in with TikTok, the application receives an access token and basic profile information (such as the display name) for the owner's own account, and the permission to upload videos. These are stored only on the owner's own computer.

Use and sharing

The data is used only to upload the owner's approved videos to the owner's TikTok account. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security and permissions, Apps and services) and delete the stored token from the local computer.
